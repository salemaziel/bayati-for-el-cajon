Issues & Policy Agenda

The campaign stands firmly against empty, vague political rhetoric — and is committed to real results. Here is exactly where we stand and how we will deliver. Eight positions, in plain language, no fine print.

Campaign priority

Prioritizing Youth Success

The world is shifting toward a multipolar structure, and in many rival nations, youth are deliberately protected from marginalization, misinformation, and neglect. Our priority must be to empower and guide young people — ensuring they have a clear, structured future to look forward to.

In our own city, nearly five out of ten students do not graduate from high school — the first step toward an educated, fulfilling life and a successful career. That is unacceptable.

I want our youth to grow up in a city where they are prioritized and uplifted — not marginalized. We are committed to creating the conditions and investments that enable your children to succeed, graduate, and build prosperous futures.

Our focus extends beyond stronger high school graduation rates. We will create more pathways for college-aged young adults to connect with paid internships, fellowships, and entry-level career opportunities that provide long-term growth and professional advancement.

By empowering the youth, we will ensure that our talent remains local, strengthening our workforce and helping small businesses grow with resilience. We are determined to build high-rises, invest boldly, and make El Cajon a bustling city once again.

✓ Measurable: graduation rates, internship placement
02
Campaign priority

Establishing Term Limits

Politicians who remain in office for a lifetime can easily become disconnected from the realities of the people they were elected to serve. Term limits are essential to prevent concentrated power, curb corruption, and ensure accountable leadership.

Gary Kendrick, at 72 years of age, has held the District 1 council seat for more than 25 years — longer than I have been alive. We have great respect for public officials who dedicate their lives to service, but we believe it is essential for leaders to move forward rather than remain in one position indefinitely.

Term limits protect accountability, promote corruption-free leadership, and make room for a new generation of scholars. Public office should be a duty to the community — not a permanent position of power.

✓ Measurable: term-limit ordinance on the agenda
03
Campaign priority

Streamlining Small Business Success

Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy — creating nearly half of all jobs and driving opportunity in communities like El Cajon. We will make support simple and accessible.

Many young people want to start businesses but lack access to clear information, funding, and guidance. Too many businesses face confusing systems, burdensome paperwork, and cash flow challenges — especially in California, where regulations can be complex and difficult to navigate.

We will focus on connecting entrepreneurs to capital, clear information, and practical tools so they can start, grow, and succeed.

✓ Measurable: business starts, loan readiness, job retention
04
Additional position

Opposing Unnecessary Taxation

Residents are already dealing with rising costs across housing, utilities, and everyday expenses — adding new taxes only increases that pressure.

We will oppose new taxation unless it is clearly necessary, has a direct local benefit, and is fully transparent to the public.

The priority is to make better use of existing funds so residents are not repeatedly asked to carry a heavier financial burden.

✓ Commitment: no new taxes without direct local benefit
05
Additional position

Protecting Seniors & Families from Fraud

Elder residents have become some of the most frequent targets of fraud — organized scams exploit trust to steal life savings. In El Cajon, fraud ranges from grandparent scams and fake legal services to large-scale embezzlement.

I will address this by funding targeted scam prevention campaigns for seniors, creating a centralized city hub for reporting and guidance, and coordinating directly with law enforcement and prosecutors to shut down repeat offenders.

By improving early detection, simplifying reporting, and strengthening accountability, we can reduce fraud before it spreads and better protect our community.

✓ Measurable: fraud reports resolved, prevention reach
06
Additional position

Flock Cameras: Protecting Safety Without Compromising Freedom

El Cajon has implemented Flock license plate reader cameras to support local public safety — but their expansion raises important questions about oversight, data sharing, and long-term impact.

Flock Safety is an independent company funded in part by major investors connected to the broader surveillance and data analytics industry, adding to concerns about how surveillance technologies may be used.

We support effective, modern policing, but we will oppose unnecessary expansion of these systems without strong transparency, accountability, and local control.

Our position is clear: protecting public safety must never come at the expense of the constitutional rights and liberties that define our community.

✓ Position: oversight, transparency, local control
07
Additional position

Putting City Services Above Partisan Politics

El Cajon residents shouldn't be paying for costly legal battles and politically driven spending that don't directly benefit the community.

We will push for stricter oversight and require clear justification before major spending decisions — ensuring city funds are directed toward public safety, infrastructure, and proven local NGOs that are actively strengthening development in our community.

✓ Measurable: spending justifications, oversight reports
08
Additional position

Cost of Living & Housing Stability

Housing in El Cajon is becoming increasingly difficult for working families to afford — as large outside investors buy up properties and drive rents beyond what local incomes can support.

When rents jump hundreds of dollars in a short period, it forces people out of the communities they've lived in for years. We will push for practical limits on bulk property purchasing, expand incentives for local ownership, and accelerate approval for attainable housing projects so supply can actually catch up.

The focus is simple: keep residents in their homes and make it realistic to live and build a future in this city.

✓ Measurable: housing approvals, local ownership, rent stability
