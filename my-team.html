The team behind the movement

Meet the people bringing decades of leadership, education, and community service to this campaign.

MK
Dr. Mark Kabban
Campaign Manager

Dr. Mark Kabban

Dr. Mark Kabban holds a doctorate in Education Leadership from Harvard University and is an accomplished educator, leadership expert, and community advocate with a lifelong commitment to empowering young people and strengthening institutions. He is the founder of YALLA (Youth and Leaders Living Actively), an organization dedicated to developing leadership and opportunity for refugee and migrant youth, and the founder of the Examined Leadership Collective, a practice focused on adult development and equity-centered leadership.

His work has earned national and international recognition, including the CNN Hero Award, Arab American "40 Under 40" honors, the Mead Fellowship, and recognition as a Top Five Young World Changer by DoSomething.org.

Across his career, Dr. Kabban has led leadership development and coaching initiatives in diverse settings — from U.S. public school systems and universities to international education networks, nonprofit organizations, and professional sports academies. His work is grounded in deliberately developmental leadership frameworks, with a focus on emotional resilience, equity, and institutional effectiveness. To date, he has coached and advised more than 100 leaders across K–12 districts, higher education, and mission-driven organizations.

Through his work, Dr. Kabban brings a powerful combination of academic rigor, real-world leadership, and a deep commitment to equity and community impact — making him a vital asset to the campaign team.

EK
Edward Kashou
Campaign Treasurer

Edward Kashou

Edward Kashou is an accomplished executive, entrepreneur, and community leader with decades of experience in business operations, finance, and strategic growth. He has built and led multiple successful enterprises across San Diego, demonstrating a consistent ability to strengthen organizations and guide them through expansion with discipline and vision.

Currently serving as a senior advisor and campaign manager, Edward brings deep operational insight and strategic leadership to the team. His background in hospitality, marketing, and large-scale operations allows him to support effective decision-making, organizational efficiency, and long-term planning.

Edward is also recognized for his longstanding commitment to community service, including more than 37 years of weekly homeless feeding initiatives in San Diego and over 30 years of service as a Board Member and Treasurer of Saint Demiana Church. In recognition of his impact, he received a Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition in 2016 presented by U.S. Representative Scott Peters.

Known for his integrity, service, and results-driven leadership, Edward remains a trusted and valued contributor to the campaign.
