— The priorities

Where the campaign stands

Three priorities anchor the agenda — youth success, term limits, and small business support — with five more positions in the full policy agenda.

01

Prioritize youth success

Nearly five out of ten students don't graduate high school in El Cajon. We'll invest in mentorship, internships, and career pathways so talent stays local.

Establish term limits

Concentrated power breeds corruption and disconnects leaders from their communities. Public office should be a duty — not a permanent seat.

Streamline small business success

Small businesses create nearly half of all jobs. We'll connect entrepreneurs to capital, clear information, and practical tools to start, grow, and succeed.

— Who is Bayati?

Raised here. Ready to serve here.

I'm Osama Bayati, a young El Cajon resident running for City Council District 1. I want our youth to grow up in a city where they are prioritized and uplifted — not marginalized.

By empowering young people, we keep our talent local, strengthen our workforce, and help small businesses grow with resilience. I'm determined to build high-rises, invest boldly, and make El Cajon a bustling city once again.
